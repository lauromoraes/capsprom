\chapter{Discussion}\label{chap:discussion}

\section{Interpretation of Results}\label{sec:discussion-interpretation}

This section will interpret the experimental findings in relation to promoter
biology, dataset heterogeneity, and representation learning.

\section{Comparison With Prior Work}\label{sec:discussion-prior-work}

Results should be compared against the qualification-stage CapsProm and
physicochemical-property studies, as well as external baselines such as CNNProm
and GraphPro when reproduced under compatible conditions.

\section{Threats to Validity}\label{sec:discussion-threats}

Potential threats include source-specific negative-set construction, class
imbalance, organism-specific signals, fixed-window assumptions, and differences
between original and reproduced baselines.

\section{Interpretability and Uncertainty}\label{sec:discussion-interpretability}

This section will discuss representation visualization, model interpretation,
and uncertainty estimation with conformal prediction when those experiments are
available.
