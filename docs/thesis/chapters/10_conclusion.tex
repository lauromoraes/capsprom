\chapter{Conclusion}\label{chap:conclusion}

\section{Contributions}\label{sec:conclusion-contributions}

At its current stage, this thesis contributes a reproducible promoter-prediction
protocol, curated and validated source-defined datasets, feature mappings for
multiple DNA representations, an explicit audit of exact-sequence leakage, and
a leakage-controlled Bhandari shuffle-control workflow. It also establishes a
ten-seed classical reference across 25 canonical source-negative datasets, with
a median paired MCC advantage of 0.0574 for XGBoost over logistic regression.
Capsule-based model evaluation remains part of the planned experimental
contribution.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion-limitations}

Current evidence is limited to classical baselines. In particular, Bhandari
provides promoter sequences only, and its synthetic shuffle controls cannot
replace evaluation against genomic non-promoters. Repeated-seed uncertainty,
paired statistical comparison, and biological-negative cross-dataset evaluation
are complete for the canonical classical baseline; broader homology auditing,
cross-source transfer, and the capsule-model experiments remain incomplete.
The exact-identity correction and Hamming-distance sensitivity audit are
complete for all 26 CDBProm, CNNProm, and GraphPro pairs, but these do not by
themselves exclude longer-range biological homology.

\section{Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion-future-work}

Future work may include broader cross-source evaluation, expanded transformer
baselines, refined attention-capsule architectures, and deeper uncertainty and
interpretability analyses.
